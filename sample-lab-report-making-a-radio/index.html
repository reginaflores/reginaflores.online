Hand-made Radio

Hand Made Radio

Here I am in college making a radio out of a Quaker Oat box. I love being in the lab, rolling up my sleeves, and getting my hands dirty. This project was especially fun for me because I was very interested in radio astronomy at the time. So, it was a way to explore the history of radio waves. I am still fascinated by the electro-magnetic spectrum. I loved radio waves so much, I even became a Ham Radio Operator; my call sign was KC2LVF
