Science Data Code

I live at the intersection of design and technology.

I am a designer, technologist and educator whose work has moved from the scale of the cosmos to the scale of DNA, and from trading floors to cultural institutions. The thread through all of it is data: how we measure the world, how we model it, and how we make it visible and useful to people.

I started in astrophysics at NASA Ames Research Center where I worked on SOFIA, an infrared observatory flown aboard a Boeing 747; later at the National Radio Astronomy Observatory where I studied the Cosmic Microwave Background; and at the National Astronomy and Ionosphere Center (Arecibo) where I studied star formation in extragalactic molecular clouds. I then spent more than five years on Wall Street turning quantitative methods into trading strategy and portfolio analysis, at Goldman Sachs and JP Morgan, before leading analytics at the Cue Group, a consultancy and insight lab.

At Parsons School of Design I turned that background toward design: algorithmic insights using The Met's digital collection, bio design and living materials, and speculative work about cities, the cosmos and what it means to be human. That period produced The Hip Hop Project at The Met Media Lab, the Smart Spirulina System with Terreform ONE, Data Selfie with NYC Media Lab and The New Museum's NEW INC, and Holobiont Urbanism with the MIT Media Lab, which went to the 2016 Venice Architecture Biennale and became a peer-reviewed paper in 2023.

I have taught code to designers at Parsons since 2015, most recently Data As Material, and now I teach AI to everyone else. Today I work as an AI consultant and educator, building data architecture and agentic AI systems with founders, organizations and cultural institutions, and I am writing a Design Manifesto for a New Earth.

A timeline

  1. Early career

    Astrophysics

    Research at NASA Ames (SOFIA), the National Radio Astronomy Observatory and Arecibo. Senior thesis: Mapping the Radio Sky. B.A. in Physics, Barnard College of Columbia University.

  2. 2005–2011

    Quantitative finance

    More than five years bridging quants and sales: Goldman Sachs, Equity Prime Brokerage; JP Morgan, Foreign Exchange, Rates and Commodities. Financial modeling, hedge fund analysis and an M.A. in Statistics from Columbia, with a thesis on the US swaps markets.

  3. 2012–2014

    Cue Group

    Director of Analytics at a consultancy, think tank and insight lab, including Mapping Youth Culture.

  4. 2014–2016

    Parsons MFA, Design and Technology

    The notebook at the core of this archive: creative code, bio design, data and physical computing. Media Lab work at The Met (metaMethods, The Hip Hop Project), the Smart Spirulina System at Terreform ONE, and a thesis on Holobiont Urbanism.

  5. 2016–2018

    Data Selfie and Holobiont Urbanism

    Data Selfie (DATA X, with Hang Do Thi Duc): NYC Media Lab Combine, NEW INC, the Solitude & ZKM Web Residencies and wide press. Holobiont Urbanism with the MIT Media Lab: Venice Architecture Biennale 2016 and the Information is Beautiful Awards longlist.

  6. 2015–today

    Teaching at Parsons

    From the MFA bootcamp to Algorithms and Simulations, Critical Computation and today's Data as Material. All courses.

  7. 2023

    Publication

    Co-author of “Holobiont Urbanism: sampling urban beehives reveals cities' metagenomes”, Environmental Microbiome.

  8. 2026

    AI consulting and education

    Agentic systems, data architecture and AI education for teams and leadership, in English and Spanish. A Design Manifesto for a New Earth in progress. Current work lives at reginafloresmir.ai.
