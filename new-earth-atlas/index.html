New Earth Atlas

A Design Manifesto for a New Earth.

Rewrites in process for the age of AI. It moves in four parts: from the cosmos, to the planet, to the technosphere we are building, and finally to the work of designing a new Earth. This atlas maps its working themes. They are evolving quickly, as AI’s acceleration opens new possibilities for the futures ahead.
