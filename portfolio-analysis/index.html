Unfortunately, due to rigid corporate intellectual property rights and regulatory controls at Goldman, I was never able to take my actual work with me when I left the firm. The below report is an example of the kind of analysis we produced. I am not the author of this particular report, it serves only to demonstrate the kind of work I did.

During my tenure at Goldman, Sachs I worked in Prime Brokerage servicing the some of the top Hedge Funds in the world. I worked in the Product Development group that served as the buisness liaison between clients and the IT staff that coded the products. It was our job to: 

  1. Understand our clients needs, how they operated and what information was important to them
  2. Design front-end and analytical products and
  3. Work with IT to make sure that the back end systems functioned correctly.
